What does Kitlane do today?

Kitlane organizes supported installed apps, lets you search by name or publisher, shows availability for a small bundled catalog, and connects account-owned devices for explicit library sharing. It’s in active development.

Does it install or update my apps?

No. The current app scans and organizes your library. Installation, updates, and AI suggestions are not enabled. Compatibility proposals are for review only.

Which computers are supported?

The first packaged preview targets Windows x64. macOS and Linux installers are planned. Their inventory adapters have development checks; native runtime validation is still pending. See the download page for current release status.

Does every installed app appear?

Coverage depends on the platform: Windows uninstall-registry entries, macOS application bundles, and Linux Flatpak apps. Portable apps, Windows Store/MSIX apps, other users’ software, and Linux native packages are outside the current scanners.

What happens to my data?

Your inventory and preferences are saved locally. Pairing alone doesn’t upload your library. Sharing needs your consent and an explicit sync. Google sign-in is required; account credentials use your operating system’s credential store.
